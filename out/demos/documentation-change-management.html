Documentation & Change Management.

One change mapped to every document it touches: updates drafted, approvals routed, retraining triggered automatically.

“Every new product means weeks of re-writing and re-training.”

A single SOP revision is never a single change. It touches the procedures that reference it, the records built from it, the people trained against the old version and, often, a clause in a scheme you are certified to. Tracking that fan-out by hand is why documentation drifts out of date between audits.

In this walkthrough a change to a food safety procedure is followed all the way out: what it affects, who has to review it, what they said, and who needs retraining as a result. Responses are collected against the change itself rather than in a mailbox, so the state of a revision is a fact you can read rather than a question you have to go and ask.

In this walkthrough

  • One document change traced to everything it touches
  • Review and acknowledgement collected against the change itself
  • Retraining raised for the people the revision actually affects
