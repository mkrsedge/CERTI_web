Recall Readiness & Traceability.

Mock recalls run end to end. Any lot traced forward and back in minutes, with the auditor-ready record to prove it.

“Our last mock recall took two days, and we still weren’t sure.”

Traceability is only ever tested when it matters, and by then the exercise is timed. Working out where a lot went and what went into it means walking several systems and a filing cabinet, and the honest answer to whether the trace was complete is usually that nobody can be certain.

This walkthrough follows a production lot in both directions — back to the incoming materials it was made from, forward to where it shipped — and assembles the audit record from what is already held. The point is not speed for its own sake. It is that the trace is reproducible, so a mock recall measures your process rather than your filing.

In this walkthrough

  • One lot traced back to incoming materials and forward to despatch
  • The auditor-ready record assembled from existing data
  • Gaps in the trace flagged before an auditor tests it
