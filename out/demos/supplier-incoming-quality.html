Supplier & Incoming Quality.

Every COA checked against spec on arrival. Bad lots caught before your floor; suppliers risk-ranked on real performance.

“I find out a certificate lapsed only when the auditor does.”

Incoming quality fails quietly. A certificate is filed without being read, a value sits just outside spec, an approval lapses between audits. None of it announces itself, and all of it resurfaces later as a deviation, a hold or a finding. The check that would have caught it is dull, repetitive and exactly the kind of work that gets skipped under pressure.

Here every certificate of analysis is read against the specification of record as it arrives, and anything out of tolerance is raised before the lot moves. Supplier standing is then built from what actually happened across deliveries rather than from a questionnaire filled in once at approval, so the ranking reflects performance instead of intent.

In this walkthrough

  • A COA read against the spec of record at goods-in
  • Out-of-tolerance results raised before the lot reaches production
  • Suppliers ranked on delivered performance, not a one-off questionnaire
